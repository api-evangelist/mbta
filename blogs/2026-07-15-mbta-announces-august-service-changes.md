Service changes are taking place in August to support Green Line tunnel signal upgrades, accessibility improvements on the Green Line C branch and at Symphony, Orange Line track and signal upgrades, Worcester Line infrastructure work, and the MassDOT West Roxbury Parkway Bridge Replacement project.
