In October, the MBTA will accomplish work on Blue Line infrastructure revitalization, accessibility upgrades on the Green Line, critical Green Line central tunnel signal upgrades, and Commuter Rail improvements while also supporting MassDOT work on Route 24 in Taunton and as part of the West Roxbury Parkway Bridge Replacement project.
