Service changes are taking place in September to support the Ruggles Station Improvements Project, Ashmont branch bridge work, Wonderland switch work, Green Line accessibility upgrades, signal modernization within the Green Line central tunnel, Worcester Line track and signal improvements, the South Station Signal and Track Improvements Project, and MassDOT’s West Roxbury Parkway Bridge Replacement project.
